## Public Domain Status

This production uses the **Alfred Sutro English translation of 1904**, which is in the **United States public domain** under the following rationale:

- Published in 1904, well before the 1931 cutoff for US copyright expiration
- No copyright renewal on record
- Freely available through Archive.org and other digital archives

## Source Text Integrity

**Use ONLY the Sutro 1904 edition as the source text.**

Do not substitute:
- Modern annotated reprints with editorial changes
- Re-translations published after 1904
- Abridged or adapted versions
- Copyrighted scholarly editions with introductions and notes

The original French play (*Monna Vanna*) was published by Maurice Maeterlinck in 1902. While the French text is also public domain in many jurisdictions, **this adaptation works exclusively from Sutro's 1904 English translation.**

## French Original Note

Maurice Maeterlinck's original French *Monna Vanna* was first published in 1902. The play is set in a fictionalized version of late 15th-century Pisa during the Florentine siege, though the characters and plot are Maeterlinck's invention, not historical figures.

**This production does NOT translate from the French.** It adapts the existing Sutro English translation, which is the authorized text used in British and American theater productions from 1904 onward.
