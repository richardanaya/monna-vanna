# Act I: The Price

**Setting:** Pisa, inside the besieged city, Guido and Marco Colonna's residence  
**Time:** Night, during the siege  
**Principals:** Giovanna, Guido Colonna, Marco Colonna, Vedio, Borso, Torello, Pisan Seigniory elders

## Summary

Pisa is starving under Florentine siege. Guido Colonna, the city's commander, receives an impossible offer from Prinzivalle, the Florentine mercenary captain: Prinzivalle will betray Florence and deliver food to Pisa — but only if Giovanna, Guido's wife, comes to his tent alone at night, wearing only a mantle (cloak) with nothing beneath. The Pisan council is horrified. Guido is paralyzed. Marco, Guido's father, denounces the offer as dishonorable. But Giovanna, after listening in silence, makes her choice: **she will go.** She argues that one woman's dignity is a small price for thousands of lives. Despite Guido and Marco's anguish, Giovanna departs into the night, wrapped in the mantle, walking alone through Pisan streets toward the city gate and the Florentine camp beyond.
