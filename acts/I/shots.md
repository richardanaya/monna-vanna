# Act I: The Price — Shot list (serves full Sutro text)

**Do not cut dialogue to fit shots.** Shots are *coverage of spoken text*. If a speech is long, hold or intercut speaker/listener; never skip lines.

**Style on every generated frame:** Tempera Chronicle suffix + negatives from `docs/style-tempera-chronicle.md`.  
**Looks:** `looks/characters.md`.  
**Mantle:** implication only — no skin below the neck.

**Room lock (all units unless noted):** Late-15th-c Pisan palace chamber; open window on ruined country / breach; marble columns; door at back; ochre limestone, iron-grey, torch-amber; painted hard light.

**Coverage grammar (reuse every unit):**
- **M** master wide (room geography)
- **S** speaker (three-quarter / CU)
- **L** listener (held reaction)
- **I** insert (window, hands, column, door-crowd) — after or between clauses, not instead of lines
