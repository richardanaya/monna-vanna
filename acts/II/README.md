# Act II: The Tent — Units (Sutro 1904)

**Source lock:** Alfred Sutro, London: George Allen, 1904 — https://archive.org/details/monnavanna00maetiala
**Rule:** Word-for-word. Every Sutro line is spoken. No paraphrase, no cuts, no invented dialogue.
**Style:** Tempera Chronicle. Tent per Sutro stage dir: *sumptuous disorder* (silk/gold hangings, chests, furs, arms) — painted luxury, **not** romantasy glow.
**Mantle visuals:** She appears *wrapt in a long mantle*; sits *closely wrapped*. Dialogue about "clad only in her mantle" stays intact. **No skin below the neck.**

The current tent beats **do not match Sutro**. Patch on the next repo pass:

- Time is **night** (Vedio: "It is past nine"), ending at **wakening dawn** — not "dawn, the morning after" as the whole act.
- **Trivulzio is a major scene**, not a brief late walk-on. He delivers the assault-at-dawn order, is confronted with intercepted letters, stabs Prinzivalle in the face, is taken away unharmed.
- The **convoy starts during the tent scene**, at her order, while they stand on the threshold — not after she refuses him.
- Recognition is **Venice childhood / Gianello** (he was twelve, she eight; pond, pearl necklace, golden ring). Not "former friend of Guido."

## Staging approach

One tent (curtain at back). Coverage grammar same as Act I: **M** master / **S** speaker / **L** listener / **I** insert.

- A-cam: tent geography (table, chests, couch/tiger-skins, curtain, lamp).
- Hold faces through long speeches (Prinzivalle's letters; her love-of-Guido speech; the fires speech).
- Convoy = stage dir as image + sound (waggons, sheep, oxen, torches) — no extra dialogue.
- Wound/bandage is plot (he is hard to recognize; she ties the linen).
- Mantle never opens for the camera.

## Unit map

Cue lines are Sutro (light OCR cleanup).

### UNIT A — The night is mine
**Players:** Prinzivalle, Vedio  
**Action:** Letter from Trivulzio: assault at dawn or immediate arrest. "The night, at least, is mine." Trivulzio will follow. Galician guards (Hernando, Diego). Lamp. Past nine. Marco has not returned — if the offer were rejected he would be here.  
**First:** VEDIO — *"Here is a letter from the Commissioner of the Republic."*  
**Last:** (into Trivulzio's entrance) PRINZIVALLE — *"He is coming…."*

### UNIT B — Trivulzio
**Players:** Trivulzio, Prinzivalle; Vedio dismissed then recalled  
**Action:** Campanile lights. Florence's "prudence," enemies, arrest. Assault tomorrow. Prinzivalle produces intercepted letters; he will save Pisa tonight. Trivulzio draws a dagger; the blow strikes Prinzivalle's face. Prinzivalle does not kill him. "Give me your hand." / "Not yet… day of punishment." Vedio: blood. Guards take Trivulzio; no injury.  
**First:** TRIVULZIO — *"Have you observed the strange light that appears to be flashing signals from the Campanile?"*  
**Key:** PRINZIVALLE — *"This evening Pisa shall be saved…"*  
**Last:** PRINZIVALLE — *"Let them bestow him in some safe place… They answer for his safety, and shall set him free at my command."*

### UNIT C — Bandage, waggons, the shot
**Players:** Prinzivalle, Vedio  
**Action:** Mirror; wound not deep but in the face. Bandage — do not cover the eyes. Vedio will follow; Prinzivalle tells him to escape; gold in the coffers. Waggons and flocks in front of the tent. Distant gun-shot. "If they should have fired at her!" Vedio exits.  
**First:** PRINZIVALLE — *"The wound is not deep, but it has bitten into my face."*  
**Last:** PRINZIVALLE — *"Go and see."*

### UNIT D — She arrives
**Players:** Vanna, Prinzivalle  
**Action:** Vedio murmurs "Master." **Monna Vanna, wrapt in a long mantle, pauses on the threshold.** Blood on her hand; a ball touched her shoulder. Conditions, regrets, husband's consent. Why: *"Because out yonder they perish of hunger…"* *"You are clad only in your mantle?" / "Yes."* He lists the convoy; they watch it start into Pisa. She sits, closely wrapped.  
**First:** VANNA — *"I have come as you bade me."*  
**Key:** VANNA — *"I have only my sandals and this mantle."*  
**Last:** VANNA — *"It matters not."* (lamp on her eyes)

### UNIT E — Gianello
**Players:** Vanna, Prinzivalle  
**Action:** He kneels, names her. *"Who are you?"* Venice, a Sunday in June; goldsmith's pearls; pond; ring; he nearly drowned. *"It was a fair-haired child named Gianello. Are you Gianello?" / "Yes."* Bandages moved; she ties them. Twelve games; Africa; he would have known her among ten thousand.  
**First:** PRINZIVALLE — *"Giovanna! … Oh, Vanna, my Vanna … — for I, too, used to call you thus."*  
**Key:** VANNA — *"Are you Gianello?"*  
**Last:** into the love argument (she has remembered the garden).

### UNIT F — Mercy without force
**Players:** Vanna, Prinzivalle  
**Action:** She will not call this the courage of the love he claims. She loves Guido — steadier, given with open eyes; she will not break it. He: she is at his mercy; he will not take what "the ordinary lover demands"; *"I shall not touch it again."* The last act "proves nothing": no country; already condemned; tonight may save him. He will not buy a smile with a lie. She: *"Ah, Gianello, Gianello, this is worth more than love… Take it."*  
**First:** VANNA — *"How feeble and cowardly men become when they love!"*  
**Key:** PRINZIVALLE — *"You are in my tent now, and at my mercy. … I have only to say a word… But you know as well as I that the love of which I have spoken craves other things…"*  
**Last:** into the alarm (hand given / talk continues until Vedio).

### UNIT G — Fly / Pisa's fires / the brow
**Players:** Vedio, Prinzivalle, Vanna  
**Action:** Whispers. Vedio: Maladura returned; six hundred Florentines; proclaimed traitor. She: *"Come to Pisa."* He fears for her; she stands surety. *"So be it: I will go with you."* Tent thrown open: bells, bonfires, Campanile. She: *"Come, my Gianello!"* **(She kisses him on the brow.)** *"That is the only kiss I can give you."* She is faint. They go out together, he supporting her.  
**First:** VEDIO *(off)* — *"Master!"* then *"Quick, quick! Master, you must fly!"*  
**Key:** VANNA — *"That is the only kiss I can give you."*  
**Last spoken:** VANNA — *"Quick! Let us hasten, it is time. We must arrive before the joy has faded."*  
**Button:** *[They go out together, PRINZIVALLE supporting VANNA.]*
