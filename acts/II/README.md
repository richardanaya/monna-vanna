# Act II: The Tent

**Setting:** Florentine siege camp, Prinzivalle's tent  
**Time:** Dawn, the morning after Act I  
**Principals:** Giovanna, Prinzivalle, Trivulzio (briefly)

## Summary

Dawn. Giovanna arrives at Prinzivalle's tent in the Florentine camp, escorted by soldiers. Prinzivalle dismisses his men, and he and Giovanna are alone. Giovanna expects the worst, but Prinzivalle is not the villain she imagined. He reveals that the "condition" — the humiliating demand that she come wearing only a mantle — was a test of her courage and Guido's trust. He has no intention of harming her. Prinzivalle was once Guido's closest friend, and he still admires them both. He will keep his word: food wagons are already moving toward Pisa. The tent scene becomes a philosophical and emotional duel about honor, trust, loyalty, and unspoken desire. Trivulzio enters with news that Florentine commanders suspect Prinzivalle's treason. Prinzivalle must flee — he asks Giovanna to come with him. She refuses: **she will return to Guido.** Prinzivalle, heartbroken but respectful, lets her go. He gives her his cloak to cover herself for the journey back. Giovanna departs at dawn as food wagons roll toward Pisa.
