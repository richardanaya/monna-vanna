# Act III: The Refusal — Units (Sutro 1904)

**Source lock:** Alfred Sutro, London: George Allen, 1904 — https://archive.org/details/monnavanna00maetiala
**Rule:** Word-for-word. Every Sutro line is spoken. No paraphrase, no cuts, no invented dialogue.
**Style:** Tempera Chronicle. Setting: **state apartment of Guido Colonna** — lofty windows, porticos, marble columns, terrace up a great double staircase, vases of flowers, view over the town.
**Mantle:** She returns in triumph (day dress or cloak as generated from Act II exit). No nude. Brow-kiss is *spoken*, not restaged as sex.

## Director's correction to `plan/trilogy.md` Act III

The current "Refusal" beats **do not match Sutro**. Patch on the next repo pass:

- She does **not** return alone. **Prinzivalle walks beside her**, face hidden/bandaged. Torello: *"But who is the man who is walking by her side?"*
- Guido's first intent is not a private interrogation. He has **waited out the night of the contract**; now he will take his fighting men and **destroy the man**. He curses and dismisses Marco; he will "forgive" her when the man has ceased to be.
- He **misreads** her bringing Prinzivalle as a **Judith** stratagem (vengeance, public execution). He calls the crowd back to acclaim a miracle.
- She tells the **truth**: *"he did not come near me, he did not touch me. … I come from his tent as from the home of a brother."* One kiss on the brow, returned. **Marco alone** stands forth: *"I believe her!"* The crowd will not.
- Guido offers them **free passage** if she will say "the only possible truth" (that she was taken). She repeats the truth. He **condemns Prinzivalle** to the lowest dungeon.
- She then **publicly recants** (*"No, no! I have lied, I have lied!"*) to seize custody: he is *her* prey. **Whisper to Prinzivalle:** *"I belong to you, I love you! I love you, my Gianello!"* He cries *"She lies! She lies to save me."* She drowns him out.
- She demands **the key**. Entrusts him to Marco. Falls. Marco, low: *"I understand your falsehood."*
- **Last line:** VANNA — *"Yes, it has been a bad dream … but the beautiful one will begin. The beautiful one will begin."* **CURTAIN.**
- Do **not** end on "does she leave / marriage in ruins / ambiguous." The play ends on the key and the second life she will start with him.

## Staging approach

One palace apartment + terrace (stairs into the hall). Coverage: **M / S / L / I** as Acts I–II.

- Crowd is **heard then seen** (acclaim, invasion of stairs, dispersal, recall). Do not invent a street chase.
- Prinzivalle is mostly **silent** until *"She lies!"* — hold him as a bandaged presence.
- Her public "vengeance" speech is a **performance for Guido**; the whisper is the truth. Shoot both (CU whisper vs shouted lie) so the audience has what Marco has.
- Key = the act's object. Generate it. Last image: her face + the promise of the key, not a fade on Guido's self-pity.

## Unit map

Cue lines are Sutro (light OCR cleanup).

### UNIT A — His turn
**Players:** Guido, Marco; Borso, Torello present  
**Action:** Dawn. City fed. Guido: the night belonged to the buyer; now he hurls his shame from him. He will use his men; Vanna is not his while the man lives; he will curse and thrust out his father. Marco: curse me, pardon her; let me see her throw herself into your arms, then I go.  
**First:** GUIDO — *"I yielded to you, to her, to every one; but now it is only just that I should have my turn."*  
**Last:** MARCO — *"Let me, at least, be convinced … that none will remain for her who is to come. … Let me, and for the last time, see her throw herself into your arms."*

### UNIT B — The acclaim
**Players:** Marco, Borso, Torello; Guido apart at a pillar  
**Action:** Murmur becomes *"Vanna, Vanna, our Monna Vanna!"* Terrace: square black with people. Borso holds Marco back. *"She returns in triumph."* TORELLO: *"But who is the man who is walking by her side?"* Borso: he hides his face. Flowers, children, guards.  
**First:** MARCO — *"It is Vanna! … She returns!"*  
**Last:** MARCO — *"More beautiful than Judith, and purer than Lucrece!"* (flowers hurled down the stairs)

### UNIT C — She and he on the steps
**Players:** Vanna, Prinzivalle, Marco, Guido; crowd at a distance  
**Action:** They appear at the top of the steps. She into Marco's arms: *"My father, I am happy."* *"Where is Guido? … He must be the first to hear."* Guido steps forward, **repels** her, clears the hall: *"Go, all!"* Seizes Marco: *"You, too!"* To the veiled man: *"Who are you…?"* Moves to tear the bandages.  
**First:** VANNA — *"My father, I am happy."*  
**Last:** GUIDO about to tear the bandages; VANNA — *"Do not touch him!"*

### UNIT D — Named / misread as Judith
**Players:** Vanna, Guido; Prinzivalle silent; crowd recalled  
**Action:** *"It is he who saved me. … He spared me, respected me. … He comes here with me, under my protection."* She names him: *"Prinzivalle."* Guido's exaltation: stratagem, Holophernes, kisses of hatred, trap. He shouts from the terrace: *"Prinzivalle! The enemy is here!"* Marco recognizes him. Guido: she is greater than Lucrece or Judith — she brings the victim alive.  
**First:** VANNA — *"It is he who saved me."*  
**Key:** VANNA — *"Prinzivalle."*  
**Last:** GUIDO — *"And how has she done this? … Listen, she will tell!"*

### UNIT E — The truth that only Marco believes
**Players:** Vanna, Guido, Marco; crowd as jury  
**Action:** She will not be kissed until he hears. *"In all my life I have never lied… he did not come near me, he did not touch me. … I come from his tent as from the home of a brother."* *"Because he loves me."* One kiss on the brow, returned. Guido: madness / not human. He asks the people to step forth if they believe. **Marco alone:** *"I believe her!"* She: *"They have no cause to believe me; but you, you who loved me!"* She repeats: *"He did not touch me."*  
**First:** VANNA — *"Yes, I will tell you; but it is all quite different."*  
**Key:** *"I come from his tent as from the home of a brother."* / MARCO *"I believe her!"*  
**Last:** GUIDO — *"Good! … Now I know all, and all is gone from me. … you seek to save him."*

### UNIT F — The bargain he will not honor
**Players:** Guido, Vanna; crowd; guards  
**Action:** Guido's oath: they may go freely if she tells "the only possible truth." She: *"I have told you the truth. … He did not touch me."* He: she has condemned him. Guards: lowest dungeon. *"You will never see him again."*  
**First:** GUIDO — *"Hear me, all of you! I will for the last time swear an oath."*  
**Last:** GUIDO — *"That man belongs to me; take him and bind him; thrust him into the lowest dungeon beneath this hall."*

### UNIT G — The second lie / the key
**Players:** Vanna, Prinzivalle (his only line), Guido, Marco; guards, crowd  
**Action:** She throws herself among the guards: *"No, no! I have lied, I have lied!"* Public hatred; she binds him. **Whisper:** *"Be silent! He saves us… I belong to you, I love you, my Gianello!"* PRINZIVALLE: *"She lies! She lies to save me."* She claims the wound as her dagger. She invents the slow-revenge story so Guido will give her the prisoner. *"Give me the key."* Entrusts him to Marco (look: he must understand). He is taken. She falls. Marco, low: *"I understand your falsehood."* Guido: *"I never doubted."* Last: *"Give me the key. … The beautiful one will begin."* **CURTAIN.**  
**First:** VANNA — *"No, no! I have lied, I have lied!"*  
**Key whisper:** *"I love you, my Gianello!"*  
**Last spoken:** VANNA — *"Yes, it has been a bad dream … but the beautiful one will begin. The beautiful one will begin."*
